Methodology

Three axes with family-specific per-task factors

The x-axis is cost per Intelligence Index task, the y-axis is the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, and the z-axis is output tokens per task. Direct per-task measurements are supplied for GPT-5.6 Sol Max, Luna Max, Terra Max, and both gpt-oss-120b settings.

For GPT-5.6 None through Xhigh, measured family totals are converted into per-task coordinates using the corresponding family Max total-to-task ratios:

Sol: cost × 0.000436427191014 · tokens × 0.000240619515594Luna: cost × 0.000298996791192 · tokens × 0.000151282505301Terra: cost × 0.000388130127292 · tokens × 0.000202805290573

The site preserves each source label. Sol lower levels remain marked Extrapolated; Luna and Terra lower levels are marked Calculated; family Max and gpt-oss points are marked Measured.

Hosted price is not self-hosted cost

The gpt-oss values use the Artificial Analysis hosted-provider cost basis. Since gpt-oss-120b is open weight, actual self-hosted economics depend on hardware utilization, energy, serving software, batching, and operations.

Use this explorer as a benchmark economics comparison, not as a universal infrastructure cost estimate.
