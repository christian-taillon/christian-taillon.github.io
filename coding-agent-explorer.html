Sticker price is not the whole cost story.

A low sticker price can make a model such as DeepSeek V4 Pro (high) look like a value play. But some models, including Kimi K2.6 and GLM-5.1, brute-force their way to higher intelligence by producing far more tokens. Prompting can curb some of that verbosity, but doing so can also reduce the deep reasoning that produces their intelligence. That tradeoff is acceptable for many tasks. It is often unacceptable for agentic workloads such as coding, where long-running processes may require repeated context-window compression and careful context management.

Even when large token windows are unnecessary, some models complete the same work with far fewer tokens and can therefore deliver a lower effective expense per task.
